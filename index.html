Project LIGTAS-AGAD

Landslide Database & Geospatial Risk Assessment System

This Landslide database is developed and maintained by DOST Funded Project LIGTAS-AGAD team with partnership with DENR-MGB, PHIVOLCS, UPLB-IRNR and implemented by UPLB-SESAM.

INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERS & COLLABORATORS
KEY SYSTEM CAPABILITIES & INTEGRATED FEATURES

GPS Accuracy & Margin of Error

High-precision geolocation with live GPS uncertainty radius circles (±meters) and 3-tiered confidence rating (High ≤ 30m, Moderate 31-100m, Approximate > 100m).

3 km Proximity Scan & Extent Auto-Filter

GPS locator automatically engages map extent filtering, plots a 3 km proximity buffer, tallies nearby historical landslides, and alerts to the nearest hazard distance.

Interactive GIS & Hazard Layers

Dual Satellite/OSM base maps with official MGB Susceptibility layers (High, Moderate, Low hazard zones) and interactive incident markers.

9-Point Multi-Criteria Filtering

Real-time filtering by Year, Region, Province, Trigger Event, General Source, Image Verification, GPS Availability, and Map Extent.

Dynamic Statistical Intelligence

Live charts for yearly frequency trends, provincial exposure distributions, trigger shares, and 6-point completeness auditing.

Official Incident Reports & Printout

Comprehensive dossiers with coordinates, casualty records, topography details, site imagery, and standardized A4 printout formatting.

Open Data Interoperability (CSV & GeoJSON)

One-click filtered data exports in CSV and GeoJSON formats for GIS analysts, researchers, LGUs, and disaster response teams.

Universal Multi-Screen Adaptation

Segmented view switcher (Map Only, Split View, Feed Only), responsive filter carousel with arrows, and 4 theme modes.
